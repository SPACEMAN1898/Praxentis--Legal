CliniRehab — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026  ·  Praxentis / Spine Tech AU

Plain-language summary: CliniRehab is a home rehabilitation app prescribed to you by your clinic. It asks you questions about your back symptoms, gives you an exercise program, and records how you go. That information is health information. It is stored on your device and in a secure Praxentis database located in Sydney, Australia, and it is shared with the clinicians at your treating clinic so they can supervise your program. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and there are no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

1. Who we are

CliniRehab is developed and operated by Praxentis (trading as Spine Tech AU), an Australian allied-health software company based in Adelaide, South Australia. We build CliniRehab for chiropractic and allied-health clinics so that their patients can follow a clinician-supervised rehabilitation program between appointments.

Praxentis is bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), including the additional protections that apply to health information.

For privacy enquiries, contact: privacy@praxentisai.com.au

2. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Patients who use the CliniRehab app on their own phone, or on a tablet provided by their clinic.
  • Practitioners at a clinic that has adopted CliniRehab, who use the practitioner mode in the app or the CliniRehab practitioner portal to supervise patient programs.

Your clinic (for example, the clinic that prescribed CliniRehab to you) remains your health service provider. Its own privacy policy governs your clinical record, your appointments, and the treatment relationship. This policy covers what CliniRehab and Praxentis do with the information that passes through the app.

3. What information we collect

About you

  • Name and date of birth — your first name, surname, and date of birth, used to identify you in the app and to match your CliniRehab record to your record at your clinic.
  • Your rehabilitation goals — the goals you enter during onboarding.
  • Account details (optional) — if you choose to create an account so your program follows you to a new phone, we collect your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash; we cannot read it.
  • Clinic record reference — once matched, the identifier of your patient record in your clinic's practice-management system (Cliniko).

Health information

  • Questionnaire answers — your answers about where your symptoms are, how your back problem behaves, which positions give relief, and related questions used to select a program.
  • Safety screening answers — your answers to screening questions (for example, about pregnancy or newly developed symptoms) that decide whether it is safe to start or continue a program without a clinician reviewing first.
  • Program and classification results — the program pattern and level the app selected or your practitioner prescribed, and the exercise list within it.
  • Exercise and progress records — which exercises you completed, partially completed, or could not do; how you felt afterwards; daily "better / same / worse" check-ins; and your answers to progression and completion reviews.
  • Practitioner decisions — any review, override, clearance, or program change made by a practitioner, with the practitioner's identity, the date, and any note they entered.

About practitioners

  • Display name, email address, and a password for the practitioner account.
  • A per-practitioner unlock code, stored on the clinic tablet only as a salted, iterated hash so that a decision made offline can still be attributed to the person who made it.
  • An audit trail of the clinical decisions each practitioner records in the app.

Technical information

  • A random account identifier created when the app first runs (see section 6).
  • The app version, and a record of each synchronisation between your device, our database, and your clinic's Cliniko account (what was sent and when — not the content).

We collect only the information needed to run your rehabilitation program. The app does not ask for, and cannot access, your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data held by your phone's operating system (such as Apple Health or Google Fit).

4. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • select and deliver the rehabilitation program your clinic has prescribed;
  • apply the safety and progression rules in the app, including pausing a program when a screening answer requires a practitioner to review it;
  • let the clinicians at your clinic see your progress, review flags, and adjust your program;
  • write a plain-language rehabilitation summary into your record at your clinic;
  • keep your program available if you reinstall the app or change devices (where you have created an account);
  • maintain an accurate, tamper-evident history of what the app recommended and what your practitioner decided.

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

5. Where your information is stored and who can see it

On your device

Your program and records are stored on your device first, so the app works without a connection. They are held in the device's secure keychain (iOS) or keystore (Android), not in plain files.

In the Praxentis database

When a connection is available, your records are copied to a database operated by Praxentis on the Supabase platform. The database is located in Sydney, Australia. Access is enforced by row-level security in the database itself: your patient account can read only your own records, and practitioner accounts can read the records of patients at their clinic and write their clinical decisions. Praxentis staff have administrative access to the database for the purposes of operating, securing, and supporting the service, and are bound by confidentiality obligations. Supabase, Inc. hosts the infrastructure as our service provider under its data-processing terms.

At your clinic (Cliniko)

Where your clinic has connected its Cliniko account, CliniRehab writes a treatment note titled "Rehabilitation Program Summary" into your patient record in Cliniko. The summary contains only information already recorded in the app — your current program and level, recent adherence, review status, and any practitioner decisions. It does not contain diagnoses or interpretations generated by the app. To find your record, the app searches Cliniko using your name and date of birth. Your clinic's Cliniko credentials are held in the clinic device's secure keychain and are used only to communicate with Cliniko; they are never sent to Praxentis.

Information stored in Cliniko is governed by your clinic's privacy policy and by Cliniko's privacy policy.

Overseas disclosure

We host your information in Australia and do not intentionally transfer it overseas. Our infrastructure provider may access systems from outside Australia for support and platform operations, subject to its contractual obligations to us.

6. Accounts

The first time the app runs it creates an anonymous account: a random identifier with no name, email, or password attached. This lets your records be saved and synchronised without asking you to sign up. An anonymous account is tied to that one device.

You may later link an email address and password to that same account so your program follows you to a new device. Linking keeps the same identifier, so nothing is copied or duplicated. If the project requires it, our authentication provider will send you an email to confirm your address. Signing out returns the app to a fresh anonymous session.

7. Practitioners and clinical oversight

CliniRehab is a clinician-supervised program, not a self-treatment tool. Practitioners at your clinic can see your questionnaire answers, screening outcomes, program, and progress, and can override the app's recommendation, clear a safety flag, or change your level. Every such decision is recorded with the practitioner's identity and cannot be edited or deleted from a device afterwards. The app never makes a diagnosis and never lifts a safety block on its own.

Practitioners using CliniRehab remain individually responsible for their professional conduct in accordance with their AHPRA board's guidelines.

8. What we do not collect or do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs are included in the app.
  • No advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or location data are collected.
  • No third party receives your health information other than your clinic (via Cliniko) and our hosting provider (Supabase) as described above.
  • Your questionnaire answers, goals, and health information are never sent to advertising or general-purpose analytics systems.
  • We do not use your information to train artificial-intelligence models.

9. Security

  • All network traffic between the app, the Praxentis database, and Cliniko is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Data at rest in the Praxentis database is encrypted by the hosting platform.
  • On-device records and credentials are held in the platform keychain/keystore.
  • Access to records is enforced by database row-level security, not only by the app's screens.
  • Clinical history in the database is append-only: past entries cannot be rewritten or erased from a device.
  • Development and test environments are kept separate from production, and real patient data is not used in development.

If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

10. Retention and deletion

Because CliniRehab records form part of your rehabilitation history at your clinic, we retain them for as long as your clinic's program with you is active and afterwards for the period your clinic is required to keep health records under South Australian and Commonwealth law (generally seven years from the last entry, or until a patient who was a minor turns 25).

You can:

  • Remove the app from your device, which deletes the records held in the app's storage. (On iOS, items in the keychain can persist until your account is deleted or the device is reset.)
  • Ask us to delete your account and records by emailing privacy@praxentisai.com.au. We will delete your records from the Praxentis database, except where we are required by law to keep them or where they have already been written into your clinic's record, which is managed by your clinic.

11. Access, correction, and complaints

You may ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing the address above. We will respond within 30 days. Corrections to your clinic record should be raised with your clinic.

If you believe we have mishandled your information, contact us first and we will investigate. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

12. Patients under 18

CliniRehab is prescribed by a clinician and is not offered directly to children. Where a patient is under 18, the program is set up through their treating clinic with the consent of a parent or guardian, and this policy applies to the information recorded about that patient.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and, where practical, notify you through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
